Module 02: Critical LXX Editions & Scholar Standards

Septuagint Critical Editions: Göttingen vs. Rahlfs-Hanhart vs. Cambridge

The exact hierarchy of Greek Old Testament critical editions utilized by the SBL, NRSVue, NETS, and modern academia.

SBL / NRSVue GOLD STANDARD

Göttingen Septuaginta (Editio Maior)

Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht (1931–Present) • Multi-Volume

Status: The supreme critical monument of Septuagint scholarship. Prepared by the Septuaginta-Unternehmen of the Göttingen Academy of Sciences (Alfred Rahlfs, Joseph Ziegler, Robert Hanhart, John Wevers, Udo Quast).

Method: Full historical-critical eclecticism reconstructing the earliest attainable Greek archetype (Ur-Septuaginta), complete with a massive two-tiered apparatus recording all manuscript groups (Alexandrian, Lucianic/Antiochene, Hexaplaric/Origenian, Catena), daughter versions, and patristic citations.

NRSVue / SBL Use: Explicitly mandated as the primary text for all books where published.

Standard Handausgabe (Manual Edition)

Rahlfs-Hanhart Septuaginta (2006)

Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft • Revised by Robert Hanhart

Status: The standard one-volume desk reference. Revision of Alfred Rahlfs's 1935 Handausgabe.

Method: Semi-critical text based primarily on the three great fourth-century uncials: Codex Vaticanus (B), Codex Sinaiticus (ℵ), and Codex Alexandrinus (A), with a minimal critical apparatus.

Role in Translation: Used as the fallback base for modern English translations (NRSVue, NETS, NABRE) for biblical books where the Göttingen editio maior has not yet appeared (e.g., Samuel/Kings, Chronicles).

Diplomatic & Historic Editions

Larger Cambridge & Editio Sixtina

Brooke-McLean (1906–1940) • Rome (1587)

Larger Cambridge Septuagint (Brooke-McLean): Premier diplomatic edition printing the exact text of Codex Vaticanus (B) with an exhaustive apparatus of other uncials/papyri (Genesis through Tobit/Judith/Esther).

Editio Sixtina (1587): Historic Catholic edition commissioned by Pope Sixtus V based on Vaticanus; standard text for 300+ years and base of Douay-Rheims OT Deuterocanon and Brenton (1844).

Module 03: Modern Münster CBGM Mechanics

The Coherence-Based Genealogical Method (CBGM) in NA28 & ECM

How the INTF (Münster) dismantled Hortian stemmatics using computer-assisted global genealogical coherence.

1. Pre-Genealogical Coherence
Statistical percentage of agreement between any two manuscripts (W1, W2) across all shared variation units.
2. Local Stemma
Determining internal transcriptional priority (a -> b or b -> a) at a single isolated variation unit.
3. Potential Ancestors
A witness is a potential ancestor of another if it contains a significantly higher ratio of prior (older) readings.
4. Global Coherence
Checking local stemma decisions against the entire manuscript network to eliminate circular genealogical loops.
